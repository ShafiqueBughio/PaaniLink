How PaaniLink Works

1

Register & Verify

Sign up with your CNIC, phone, and delivery address. Apply for subsidised status if eligible.

2

Book a Tanker

Select tanker size, hydrant, and schedule. Commercial bookings are auto-approved; subsidised go for officer review.

3

Dispatch & Track

Hydrant operator assigns a driver and tanker. Track your delivery status in real-time.

4

Confirm Delivery

Once water is delivered, confirm receipt or raise a dispute. Full history kept for every trip.

Our Services

What PaaniLink Offers

End-to-end water tanker management for residents, officers, and operators.

  • 01 Subsidised Deliveries

    Registered low-income households, hospitals, and mosques get water at a fraction of the commercial rate.

    Book Now

  • 02 Quota Management

    Each hydrant has a daily water quota. The system ensures no hydrant exceeds its limit, preventing shortages.

    Learn More

  • 03 Real-Time Tracking

    From booking to confirmation, every delivery step is logged. Customers see live status updates.

    Track Order

  • 04 Role-Based Access

    Customers, Zone Officers, Hydrant Operators, Drivers, and Admins each see only what they need.

    Login

  • 05 Dispute Resolution

    Customers can flag incomplete or incorrect deliveries. Zone Officers investigate and resolve disputes.

    Learn More

  • 06 City-Wide Reports

    Administrators get full visibility — delivery rates, revenue, quota consumption, and subsidy usage.

    Dashboard
